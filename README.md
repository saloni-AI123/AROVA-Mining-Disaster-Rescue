# AROVA – AI-Assisted Autonomous Mining Rescue System

> **AROVA** is an AI-assisted mining disaster rescue system that combines drone-based aerial sensing, AI hazard analysis, risk prediction, and rover-based ground inspection to support safer, faster, and better-informed rescue operations.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Mining disaster zones can be extremely dangerous and difficult to access. After an accident, rescue teams may not have complete information about the actual condition of the affected area before entering.

Fire, high temperatures, hazardous gases, collapsed structures, debris, blocked paths, unstable areas and poor visibility can make direct inspection risky for rescue personnel.

The major challenge is to understand the disaster environment, identify dangerous areas, locate possible victims and assess the risk **before rescue personnel enter the affected zone**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AROVA** combines drones, AI-based analysis and ground rovers into an integrated mining disaster rescue system.

The drone first performs an aerial survey of the affected area and collects real-time **RGB, thermal, GPS and mapping data**. This information is processed using AI to identify possible victims, heat sources, hazards, blocked paths and other dangerous conditions.

The system then estimates the risk level of different areas and provides a risk-aware view of the disaster zone.

Based on the available risk information, a ground rover can be used to inspect selected areas and collect additional **visual, thermal and environmental data**.

The drone and rover information is combined at the control system to provide the mission controller with better situational awareness and decision support.

When a critical-risk condition is detected, AROVA can generate an alert and provide relevant information to the rescue team before they enter the affected zone.

> **AROVA does not replace human decision-making. AI analyzes the available information, predicts risk and assists the mission controller, while critical rescue decisions remain under human supervision.**
